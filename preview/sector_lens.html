Structural long-run sector behavior · current monthly tilt recalculated from fundamentals
Infrastructure, power & capital goods is highly cyclical and capex-led. Order inflows track the government and private capital-expenditure cycle and are sensitive to interest rates because projects are long-gestation and debt-funded. Execution has a strong seasonal pattern: activity slows through the monsoon (Q2, Jul–Sep) and accelerates into H2, peaking in the Jan–Mar fiscal year-end push. Leverage makes the sector an early beneficiary of easing cycles and an early casualty of tightening ones.
